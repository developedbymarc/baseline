# Resting heart rate (bpm)

## Prepare
- Sit or lie down and rest for 5 minutes.
- Stay calm and still. No talking.
- Skip caffeine and exercise for 30 minutes before.

## Take the reading
- Watch or tracker: wait until the number settles, then read it.
- By hand: put two fingers on the thumb side of your wrist. Count beats for 30 seconds and double it.

## Enter
- One whole number in bpm.

## Retake
- The app only accepts 25-220 bpm. Outside that, retake.
- Retake if you moved, talked or felt stressed during the reading.
- Retake if the number jumped around.
