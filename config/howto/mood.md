# Mood (self-rating, 1-5)

## Prepare
- Take a moment and think about how you feel right now.
- Don't overthink it. Go with your first answer.

## Take the reading
- There is no device. You rate yourself.
- 1 is very low. 3 is okay. 5 is very good.

## Enter
- One whole number from 1 to 5.

## Retake
- The app only accepts 1-5. Re-enter if you typed anything else.
- Re-enter if you tapped the wrong number.
