# Blood pressure (mmHg)

Two numbers: systolic (top) and diastolic (bottom). Enter both.

## Prepare
- Sit for 5 minutes with your back supported and feet flat.
- No caffeine or exercise for 30 minutes before.
- Empty your bladder first.

## Take the reading
- Put the cuff on your bare upper arm.
- Rest your arm on a table so the cuff is at heart level.
- Stay still and silent while the cuff inflates.

## Enter
- Systolic in mmHg, for example 118.
- Diastolic in mmHg, for example 76.

## Retake
- Systolic must be 50-260 and diastolic 30-150. Outside that, retake.
- Retake if systolic is not higher than diastolic. You may have swapped them.
- Wait 1 minute, then retake if you moved or talked.
