# Sleep duration (hours)

## Prepare
- Log it in the morning, while you still remember the night.

## Take the reading
- Wearable: use the total sleep time it shows.
- No wearable: estimate from when you fell asleep to when you woke up.
- Take off time you spent awake in bed.

## Enter
- Hours slept last night, for example 7 or 7.5.

## Retake
- The app only accepts 0-16 hours. Outside that, re-enter.
- Re-enter if you typed minutes instead of hours, for example 450.
- Re-enter if you entered the wrong night.
