# Body temperature (°C)

## Prepare
- Wait 30 minutes after eating, drinking, exercise or a hot shower.
- Rest and stay in a room at normal temperature.

## Take the reading
- Follow your thermometer's instructions.
- Oral: place the tip under your tongue, mouth closed.
- Ear or forehead: point at the right spot and hold still.
- Wait for the beep.

## Enter
- A number in °C, for example 36.8.
- If your thermometer shows °F, convert it: subtract 32, then divide by 1.8.

## Retake
- The app only accepts 30-43 °C. Outside that, retake.
- Retake if the thermometer was misplaced or you spoke during the reading.
- Retake if the value looks wrong for how you feel.
