# Exercise (minutes)

## Prepare
- Log it at the end of the day, or right after a workout.

## Take the reading
- Add up all the time you were actively exercising today.
- Walks, sports and workouts count. Rest time doesn't.
- Wearable: use its active or exercise minutes.

## Enter
- Total minutes for the day, as a whole number.
- Enter 0 if you didn't exercise.

## Retake
- The app only accepts 0-600 minutes. Outside that, re-enter.
- Re-enter if you typed hours instead of minutes, for example 1 instead of 60.
- Re-enter if you counted yesterday's exercise.
