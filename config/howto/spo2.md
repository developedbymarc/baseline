# Blood oxygen (SpO2, %)

## Prepare
- Sit and rest for 5 minutes.
- Warm your hands if they are cold.
- Remove nail polish or fake nails from the finger you use.

## Take the reading
- Clip the sensor on your index or middle finger.
- Rest your hand on your chest or a table, still and relaxed.
- Wait 30 seconds, until the number stops changing.

## Enter
- One whole number in %, for example 97.

## Retake
- The app only accepts 50-100 %. Outside that, retake.
- Retake if the number keeps jumping or the sensor lost the signal.
- Results can be 2-4% off (MedlinePlus, Pulse Oximetry), so retake once if the value surprises you.
